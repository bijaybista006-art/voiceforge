# Recording script: names and English words

For the voice **Bijay - Nepali**. 138 sentences, 6 sessions of 23, each 5–8 seconds. Every word on the list appears twice, inside a story sentence.

The same sentences come first on **Admin → Training data → Nepali → Read a sentence** (ids ne-592 to ne-729), with the session shown in the reading note. Record them there; the page saves each clip with its exact transcript.

## Before every session

- **Same mic, same distance:** about 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side. Mark the spot.
- **Quiet room:** soft furnishings, fan and AC off, phone on silent.
- **No “enhance” or noise-cancel** on the phone or mic. The backend cleans the audio itself.
- **Steady storytelling tone:** the voice you use for Nepal Unfolded narration. Not slower or louder for the English words; say them the way Nepalis say them, inside the sentence.
- **One breath per sentence**, a short pause at a comma, full stop at ।.
- **If you stumble, record that sentence again.** A clip must match its text exactly.
- **Take a 2-minute break between sessions** so your voice stays the same from the first sentence to the last.
